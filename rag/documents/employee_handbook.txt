NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. PURPOSE

This handbook describes the general workplace policies and expectations for employees of Nexora Technologies. All employees are expected to read and follow these policies.

2. WORKING HOURS

The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to work 40 hours per week. Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, including a one-hour lunch break.

Employees may request flexible starting and finishing times with approval from their direct manager.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 7 days before the requested leave date whenever possible.

Annual leave requests must be approved by the employee's direct manager. Approval depends on team availability and current business requirements.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days are forfeited unless an exception is approved by Human Resources.

4. SICK LEAVE

Employees receive up to 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible if illness prevents them from working.

A medical certificate may be requested when an employee is absent for more than 3 consecutive working days.

5. PROBATION PERIOD

New employees normally complete a 3-month probation period.

During probation, the employee's manager evaluates performance, communication, reliability, and suitability for the role.

The company may extend the probation period by up to 2 additional months when further evaluation is required.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully with colleagues, customers, and business partners.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not tolerated.

Employees must protect confidential company and customer information and must not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals.

7. COMPANY EQUIPMENT

Company laptops, phones, accounts, and other equipment are primarily provided for business purposes.

Employees are responsible for protecting equipment assigned to them and must immediately report lost, stolen, or damaged company property to the IT department.

Employees leaving the company must return all company-owned equipment before their final working day.

8. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

Formal employee performance reviews are normally conducted twice per year.

Performance reviews may consider work quality, achievement of objectives, teamwork, communication, reliability, and professional development.

Employees and managers are encouraged to discuss performance throughout the year rather than waiting for the formal review.

9. TRAINING AND PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Employees may request company support for courses, certifications, conferences, or other professional development activities relevant to their role.

Training expenses require approval from the employee's manager before registration or purchase.

10. POLICY QUESTIONS

Employees who are uncertain about a policy in this handbook should contact their manager or the Human Resources department for clarification.
