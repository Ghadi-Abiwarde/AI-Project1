NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the requirements for employees who work remotely as part of Nexora Technologies' hybrid working arrangement.

Remote work is intended to provide employees with flexibility while maintaining productivity, communication, security, and collaboration.

2. REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY

Employees become eligible for regular remote work after successfully completing their probation period.

Employees who are still within their probation period are normally expected to work from the office unless their manager approves an exception.

Remote work eligibility may depend on the employee's role, performance, responsibilities, and current business requirements.

3. REMOTE WORK DAYS

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

Remote work days should be agreed upon with the employee's direct manager.

Managers may require employees to attend the office on a normally scheduled remote day when an important meeting, training session, customer visit, or other business requirement requires physical attendance.

Unused remote work days cannot be accumulated or transferred to future weeks.

4. WORKING HOURS

Employees working remotely are expected to follow the same standard working hours that apply when working from the office.

Employees should remain reachable through approved company communication channels during working hours.

Temporary schedule adjustments may be approved by the employee's direct manager.

5. WORK LOCATION

Employees should work from a location that allows them to perform their responsibilities safely and without unreasonable interruption.

Employees must have a reliable internet connection suitable for their work.

Working remotely from another country requires prior written approval from both the employee's manager and Human Resources.

6. COMMUNICATION

Remote employees are expected to regularly check company email and approved communication platforms.

Employees must attend required virtual meetings and should inform their team when they will be unavailable for an extended period during working hours.

7. INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees must follow company information-security requirements while working remotely.

Company information must not be accessed using shared or public computers.

Employees should avoid accessing confidential company information through unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

Company-issued devices must use the security software and configuration provided by the IT department.

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents, lost devices, or unauthorized access to the IT department.

8. COMPANY EQUIPMENT

Employees may use company-issued laptops and approved accessories while working remotely.

Company equipment remains the property of Nexora Technologies and should not be used by family members or other unauthorized individuals.

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable precautions to prevent loss or damage.

9. PRODUCTIVITY AND PERFORMANCE

Employees working remotely are expected to meet the same performance standards as employees working from the office.

Remote work privileges may be reviewed if an employee repeatedly fails to meet performance, communication, availability, or security expectations.

10. POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this remote work policy require approval from the employee's direct manager and, when appropriate, Human Resources.

Employees with questions about remote work eligibility or specific circumstances should contact their manager or Human Resources.
